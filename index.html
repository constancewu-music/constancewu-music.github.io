Constance 儬雯 Wu 🐰

Historical Performance Violinist

About Performances Gallery

Constance Wu is a Taiwanese baroque and modern violinist. She holds degrees from Indiana University and The Juilliard School. She loves introducing early music to new listeners and teaches students of all ages, hoping to pass along the same genuine joy and curiosity that drew her to the violin. Outside of music, she enjoys baking desserts, crocheting, discovering cozy cafés with friends, and trying to make friends with every cat she meets.
